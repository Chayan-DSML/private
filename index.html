TourBhook by PRAMAHAR

TourBhook is an AI travel planner from Pramahar Technologies. It builds route-aware itineraries so a day in a new city stays in order, instead of jumping across town between disconnected stops.

Booking sites sell flights, hotels, and tickets. TourBhook starts after the booking. It groups nearby places into walking clusters, accounts for transit time and opening hours, and keeps the day from backtracking. Travelers set a destination, a duration, and a pace. The plan can then be saved, edited, or cloned from a curator and adjusted to new dates.

Recommendations prefer places rated 4.5 stars or higher. Review notes are summarized so the useful points are easy to scan. When a day runs long, the plan can suggest a break at a nearby cafe or restaurant. Packing notes follow the weather and the activities on the itinerary.

TourBhook does not replace a booking checkout, a receipt inbox, offline map downloads, or group expense splitting. Those jobs stay with the tools built for them. This site also publishes a privacy policy, account deletion steps, and a FAQ that compares TourBhook with booking sites, inbox organizers, and do-it-yourself map planners.
