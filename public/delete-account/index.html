Delete your TourBhook account

You can ask Pramahar Technologies to delete your TourBhook account and the personal data tied to it. You do not need to sign in to make this request.

1. How to request deletion

  1. If the TourBhook app shows a delete-account option in account settings, you can use that.
  2. If it does not, submit the form on this page. This is the web request we use when deletion is not completed inside the app.
  3. Or use the button below. It opens the mail app on this device with the subject “Account deletion request”. Include the email address or phone number on the account, and the words “Please delete my TourBhook account”.

Email a deletion request

We may reply to that email or phone number to confirm you control it before we delete the account.

2. Request form

Request received. We will confirm it using the email address you entered and delete the account data described below within 30 days.

3. What we delete

After we confirm the request, we delete:

  • Your account profile, including name, email address, phone number, and Google Sign-In identifier.
  • Trip plans, saved places, preferences, and other content stored in the account.
  • One-time verification codes, which are not kept after use.
  • Support messages that are only about that account, unless we still need them for a legal claim.

4. What we may keep, and for how long

  • Payment and tax records from Razorpay transactions, such as the amount, date, and payment reference. We keep these for up to 8 years where Indian tax or accounting law requires it. We do not keep your card number or UPI PIN.
  • Security logs for up to 90 days, to investigate abuse or fraud.
  • Backup copies until the backup cycle overwrites them, within 90 days.

Retained records are limited to what that purpose needs and are not used to operate the deleted account.

5. Timing

We complete deletion within 30 days of confirming that the request comes from the account holder. We email that confirmation to the address on the account. Deleting the account does not by itself reverse a payment that has already been captured; payment questions can be sent to the same email address.
