Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy describes how Pramahar Technologies (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores, shares, and deletes personal information when you use the TourBhook mobile app or the website at tourbhook.com.

1. Who we are

TourBhook is an AI travel planner operated by Pramahar Technologies, Bengaluru, India. For privacy requests, email hello.tourbhook@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the account, plan trips, and take payment.

  • Account details. Name, email address, phone number, and the identifier of the sign-in method you choose.
  • Google Sign-In. If you sign in with Google, we receive the name, email address, and Google account identifier that Google shares with us for that sign-in. We use this to create and recognize your TourBhook account. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Email and one-time codes. If we verify an email address or phone number, we send a one-time code through our email or SMS provider. The code is used only to confirm that you control that address or number. We do not keep the code after verification is finished.
  • Trip content. Itineraries, saved places, preferences, and other content you create in the app.
  • Payments. When you pay, Razorpay processes the payment. We receive the payment status, order or payment reference, amount, and the contact details needed to match the payment to your account. Card numbers, UPI PINs, and bank credentials are collected by Razorpay and are not stored on TourBhook servers.
  • Support and website forms. If you email us, join the waitlist, or use a form on tourbhook.com, we keep the details you submit, such as your name, email address, phone number, and message.
  • Technical data. App version, device type, IP address, and basic logs used to keep the service secure and to fix failures.

3. How we use information

  • Create and protect your account.
  • Build and show your trip plans.
  • Process payments and send receipts.
  • Reply to support requests and deletion requests.
  • Send service messages about your account or a booking. We send marketing email only where you have asked for it, and you can opt out.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, and security incidents.

We do not sell personal information.

4. Who we share information with

We share information with service providers who process it for us:

  • Google — Google Sign-In, and Google Cloud hosting for this website and its database. Google’s policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Email and SMS providers — to deliver one-time codes and service messages. They receive the address or number and the message content needed to deliver it.
  • Razorpay — to take payments. Razorpay’s policy: razorpay.com/privacy.

We also share information if a law, court, or regulator requires it, or if it is needed to investigate fraud or protect someone’s safety.

5. How we protect information

The website and app are served over HTTPS. Access to stored account and form data is limited to people who need it to operate TourBhook. One-time codes are not stored after they are used. Payment card and UPI details stay with Razorpay. No method of storage is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects your account, we will tell you as required by law.

6. How long we keep information

  • Account and trip content. While your account is open.
  • After a deletion request. We delete the account and the personal data listed on the account deletion page within 30 days of confirming the request.
  • Payment and tax records. Up to 8 years where Indian tax or accounting law requires us to keep them.
  • Security logs. Up to 90 days.
  • Backups. Remaining copies are overwritten within 90 days.
  • Website messages. Until you ask us to delete them, or for up to 24 months when we still need them to respond.

7. Deleting your account

You can ask us to delete your TourBhook account without signing in, using the steps at https://www.tourbhook.com/delete-account. You can also email hello.tourbhook@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion request”.

8. Children

TourBhook is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We will post changes on this page and update the date above. If a change affects how we use account data in a material way, we will also notify account holders by email or in the app.

10. Contact

We aim to reply within 5 business days.
